Local Agent Bridge

Your own local agents — Hermes, OpenClaw, Home Assistant — reachable from Muse, without your home data leaving the house.

What it is

Local Agent Bridge is a small open-source app you run on your own machine, plus a privacy-first relay. It lets Meta's Muse personal agent talk to the AI agents you already run locally — your home automation, your open-source assistants, your local models. Your home dials out to the relay when you ask for something; nothing ever dials in. Your data stays yours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the bridge

    Install the open-source bridge app on your own machine and point it at your local agents.

  2. 2

    Pair with Muse

    Pair it with Muse using a one-time code. The pairing token stays on your machine and can be revoked anytime by deleting it.

  3. 3

    Just ask

    Ask Muse to work with your local agents, in plain conversation.

Example prompts

  • Ask my home agent whether the front door is locked.
  • Have my local assistant check the living room camera and tell me if the cats are on the couch.
  • Tell my OpenClaw agent to download tonight's file and shut the server down after.
  • Ask Hermes for a summary of today's smart home activity.

Privacy by design

  • Your home dials out; nothing dials in. The bridge only makes outbound connections. There is no inbound port, no remote access to your machine, ever.
  • Task data is relayed and discarded. Tasks pass through the relay to reach your bridge and are then forgotten — never stored, never mined, never sold.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The relay holds no personal information. Pairing tokens live only on your machine.

Read the full privacy policy — it's short and plain-spoken.

Open source

The bridge app is open source. You can read exactly what runs on your machine, audit what it sends, and run it yourself. Transparency is the point: the bridge only works because you can verify it does nothing it shouldn't.
